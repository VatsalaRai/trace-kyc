# Competitor benchmarking
Research date: 2026-10-30. Method: official public documentation and support pages. This is **not** hands-on usability testing, and it does not rank platforms.

Cells use only what the cited sources say. "Not verified" means the evidence was insufficient, not that a feature is absent. Processing windows are platform-specific guidance and are not comparable performance measurements. No numerical usability ratings are given because no evaluation method has been defined or applied.

| Dimension | Zerodha | Groww | Upstox | Angel One |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First-time onboarding | PAN, Aadhaar/DigiLocker, IPV, e-sign documented (Z01) | PAN, Aadhaar, document submission, e-sign documented (G01) | KYC help category available; full journey not verified (U01) | Account opening and KYC process documented (A01) |
| Verification tracking | Signup portal and KRA status inquiry (Z02) | Reverification prompts documented; initial status UI not verified (G02) | Not verified (U04) | In-app application status documented (A01) |
| Error/rejection handling | Detailed rejection reasons and correction methods (Z03) | Reverification path documented (G02) | Not verified (U04) | Six issue categories and resubmission guidance (A02, A03) |
| Address modification | Online and offline procedures documented; online route uses e-sign (Z04, Z05) | Support request followed by in-app modification (G03) | Address-change help category and an online DigiLocker-based address change article documented (U02, U06) | Online in-app address update for individual accounts, using Aadhaar fetched from DigiLocker, then e-sign (A04, A05) |

## Evidence ledger
- **Z01** Zerodha · First-time onboarding: Online onboarding includes PAN, Aadhaar/DigiLocker, IPV and e-sign. Confidence: High. Evidence type: Official public documentation. Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: [Account opening guide](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-online/articles/how-do-i-open-an-account-online). Support check: Partial: Aadhaar/DigiLocker and e-sign are on this page; PAN and IPV were seen on related Zerodha pages.
- **Z02** Zerodha · Verification tracking: Applicants can track status through the signup portal; KRA verification may delay activation. Confidence: High. Evidence type: Official public documentation. Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: [Tracking and timelines](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-online/articles/how-do-i-know-when-my-account-is-open-how-long-does-it-take). Support check: Partial: portal tracking and the 48-working-hour ticket guidance were seen. The KRA dependency is stated on a related Zerodha page (see also).
- **Z03** Zerodha · Error/rejection handling: Specific documentation issues have corresponding correction instructions. Confidence: High. Evidence type: Official public documentation. Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: [Application on hold](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/online-account-opening/online-account-opening-process/articles/why-has-my-application-been-rejected). Support check: Supported: the page lists rejection reasons with corrections.
- **Z04** Zerodha · Address modification: Registered address can be modified online for eligible accounts, with verification requirements. Confidence: High. Evidence type: Official public documentation. Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: [Online address modification](https://support.zerodha.com/category/your-zerodha-account/account-modification-and-segment-addition/articles/how-do-i-change-the-registered-address-on-my-account-online). Support check: Partial: online e-sign steps seen; "eligible accounts" wording not seen. Note: Online steps with e-sign are documented. The "eligible accounts" wording from the original report was not seen in the excerpt read.
- **G01** Groww · First-time onboarding: PAN/Aadhaar, document submission and e-sign are part of account setup. Confidence: High. Evidence type: Official public documentation. Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: URL not yet confirmed. Support check: Not checked: no source located.
- **G02** Groww · Verification tracking: Unconfirmed KYC can trigger a "Verify Now" flow; guidance lists possible causes. Confidence: High. Evidence type: Official public documentation. Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: [KYC reverification](https://groww.in/help/my-account/ma-kyc/why-do-i-need-to-reverify-my-kyc--63). - **G03** Groww · Address modification: Address modification begins with customer support and proceeds through an in-app prompt. Confidence: High. Evidence type: Official public documentation. Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: [Address changes](https://groww.in/help/my-account/searchable/how-can-i-change-my-address--54). Support check: Supported: support request, then an in-app prompt.
- **U01** Upstox · First-time onboarding: Online onboarding includes mobile OTP, KYC/bank verification and e-sign. Confidence: High. Evidence type: Official public documentation. Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: URL not yet confirmed. Support check: Not checked: no source located.

Accessed 2026-10-30. Source: [Profile updates](https://help.upstox.com/support/solutions/folders/277909). ## Limitations
- Findings come from documentation, not observed live screens.
- Confidence describes how strongly the cited page supports the observation, not how usable the platform is.
- Some URLs were located in a later sourcing pass and are marked in the registry; pages were not each re-read in full.
- Entries marked supplementary were added during sourcing and were not in the original report.
